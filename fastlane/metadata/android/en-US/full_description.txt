Sakuro is a privacy-friendly local video player for Android with real-time image enhancement.

Features:
* Browse videos stored on your device or open a file through the system picker
* Hardware-accelerated playback based on AndroidX Media3
* Configurable shader chains for upscaling, denoising and sharpening
* Presets for anime, cartoons and live-action video
* Automatic content classification and adaptive quality controls
* Audio and subtitle track selection, external subtitles and playback speed controls
* Gesture controls and picture-in-picture playback
* Technical playback statistics and an on-device benchmark for advanced users

Sakuro is local-first. It contains no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, cloud sync or crash-reporting SDKs. The F-Droid build does not request Internet access. Video library data, settings and presets stay on the device.

The application is free software released under GNU GPL v3.0.
